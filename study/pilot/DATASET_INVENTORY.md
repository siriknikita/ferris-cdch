## What the pilot validates

- The verified DIV2K archive contained 100 RGB PNG files. No embedded ICC profiles were observed. Decoded source pixel hashes and center-reference crop hashes were unique. A thumbnail review found predominantly photographic imagery and no clear synthetic-image series. Per-image sensor provenance, near duplicates, and retouching history remain unresolved.
- All 100 originals, 100 LR inputs, 100 bicubic controls, 100 SwinIR and 100 Real-ESRGAN outputs were generated and audited. Every source had all five variants within one split. The 400 classifier inputs passed mode, dimension, format, and PNG-metadata checks. PNG bytes were hashed and verified before training.
- Original is the released HR reference. LR is the local Pillow bicubic downsample. Bicubic is conventional interpolation from that LR. SwinIR and Real-ESRGAN are learned reconstructions from the same LR. PNG re-encoding gives these conditions common storage without retroactively equalizing acquisition history.
- The release pages for the prioritized datasets document proprietary, separate, or inferred historical processing. This study used a self-generated, checksum-bound SR pairing protocol because the verified releases did not establish distributed learned reconstructions with the required paired provenance and controls.

3. Obtain authorized FF++ c23 and Celeb-DF v2 video data and stage blind manifests through a separate custodian. Record official split versions and how crop failures and unmatched videos are counted.
